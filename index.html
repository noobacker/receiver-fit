Why not the offensive line? On real plays, late openers on weak-line teams got open, got targeted and caught the ball no worse than on strong-line teams. The QB's timing is the mismatch that shows up.
Limits. 8 weeks of 2021 data, about 40 to 70 man routes per player, so single scores have wide error. Part 2 is a grounded estimate validated on 7 teams and 28 receivers, not a proven prediction. "Open" means 3+ yards past the line and 3+ yards from every defender, at least 0.5 s after the snap.
