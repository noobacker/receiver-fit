      Part 2 Would he fit?

      If he joined another team

      Quarterbacks throw at different speeds. A fast thrower needs receivers who get open early. We check how often he would already be open by the time that team's QB lets go. Change the team in the top bar.

      Is he open by the time the QB throws?

      The vertical line is how long this team's QB usually waits before throwing

      This means "open in time", not "more passes thrown to him". In our data, being open early did not reliably lead to more targets.

      Limits. Only 8 weeks of 2021 games, so one player has just 40 to 70 routes against man coverage and his score can be off. Part 2 is an informed estimate, tested on 7 teams, not a guarantee. "Open" means at least 3 yards past the line and 3 yards from every defender.
